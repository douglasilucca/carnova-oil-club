SMS Terms & Conditions

Terms for the Carnova Oil Club transactional messaging program.

Program

Carnova Oil Club sends transactional and customer care messages related to your Oil Club purchase, membership, digital membership access, vehicle registration, service, appointments, and other membership-related account notifications.

Opt-in and consent

Customers opt in through the Carnova Oil Club web purchase form. Consent is optional and is not required to purchase a membership. Message frequency varies based on membership and service activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Managing messages

Reply STOP to opt out. After STOP, no further non-essential program SMS should be sent unless you later opt in again. Reply HELP for assistance. Carrier delivery is not guaranteed.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for providing a valid mobile number and for ensuring you are authorized to use it. Keep your membership link secure.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for information about data handling and SMS privacy.

Support

For support about your membership or these terms, contact Carnova Oil Club through the contact information provided on our website or membership communications.
