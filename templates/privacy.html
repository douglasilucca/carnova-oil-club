CARNOVA OIL CLUB

Privacy Policy

How Carnova Oil Club handles information used to provide memberships and service.

Effective September 3, 2026

Information we may collect

We may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, Oil Club membership information, vehicle information when you register a vehicle, and transaction-related data needed to provide our services.

How we use information

We use information to fulfill Oil Club memberships, provide digital membership access, support vehicle registration, schedule and manage service appointments, communicate about membership and service activity, and send transactional SMS when you have opted in.

Mobile information and SMS privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are not sold. They are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers necessary to provide the requested service, such as SMS delivery, payment processing, hosting, and related operational providers.

SMS frequency varies based on membership and service activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Opting out of SMS does not cancel your Oil Club membership, and SMS consent is not required as a condition of purchase.

Service providers

Operational providers may process information on our behalf to support payment, hosting, communications, security, and membership services. We do not authorize these providers to use your information for their own marketing through our SMS program.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. We retain information as needed to provide memberships and services, maintain records, resolve issues, comply with applicable requirements, and support legitimate business operations.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Carnova Oil Club through the contact information provided on our website or membership communications.
